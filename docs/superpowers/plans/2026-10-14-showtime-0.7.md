# showTime 0.7.0 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (inline). Steps use `- [ ]`.

**Goal:** Ship 0.7.0 "look and sound": sound packs and custom sound files, highlight colours and
spotlight styles, click animations, a watermark, caption fonts/styles and embedded font files,
per-step voices and `showtime voices`, studio controls for them.

**Architecture:** Same layers. Pure: `src/audio/sfx.ts` (packs), `src/timeline/build.ts`
(clickFx, spotlight kinds), `src/voices.ts` parsing. I/O: `src/assets.ts` (font and image files
to data URIs), `src/voices.ts` listing, pipeline embedding.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-showtime-0.7-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Presentation only: the action hash and 0.6 takes are unchanged; `sounds: true` sounds as before.
- Defaults reproduce the 0.6 look (ripple, dim spotlight, dark pill, `style.font`).
- `src/timeline/**`, `src/audio/mix.ts`, `src/audio/sfx.ts` stay pure.
- Every user-visible error starts with what failed and says what to do.

## Review Focus

1. A font file that fails to load in the renderer: the render must not hang (delayRender) and must
   say which font.
2. Large assets: a 4.9 MB font and a 2 MB watermark image embedded in `timeline.json` keep the
   studio and renders working (the studio sends the timeline on every state).
3. `show.voice` overriding the engine on one line (system → openai) without a key: the error names
   the step.
4. Click effects when two clicks fall in one step (type steps click first, then type) and on steps
   without a click.
5. Old timelines/scripts: `sounds: true`, `spotlight: true`, no `click` — identical to 0.6 output.
